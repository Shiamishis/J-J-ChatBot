 Decision-Making Playbook
How to move from a weak engagement page to a prioritized action plan using filters, drilldowns, and
 KPI interpretation.
This guide is designed for realistic end-user enablement on the Customer Engagement Dashboard in the NovaCarta EMEA
dummy dataset. It follows the same training intent as the Executive Decision Playbook: help users move from a red dashboard
signal to a focused, practical response. The examples are grounded in the customer-engagement KPI set, where Digital Channel
Coverage can be on or near target while Event Follow-up, Promotional Email Effectiveness, Interaction Frequency, or
Multi-Channel Engagement Score reveal where value is actually leaking.

1. First five clicks: a disciplined start to analysis
1. Set the fiscal quarter. 2. Read the KPI strip. 3. Check digital coverage by market. 4. Read the multi-channel score
trend. 5. Open the scorecard only after the top-line engagement story is clear.
Starting with one market, one event, or one email campaign too early can lead to random hunting. Starting with the engagement
cards and the top-line visuals preserves leadership discipline: first size the problem, then isolate the type of engagement
weakness, then localize ownership.

2. Decision pattern: reach is healthy, but orchestration is weak
In the FY2025 Q4 slice, Digital Channel Coverage is above target at 51.2% versus a 50.0% target, but Multi-Channel
Engagement Score is only 2.3 against a 7.5 target. This is the classic signal that the organization is achieving reach
but not enough coordinated depth across channels.
1. Look for KPIs that are green. 2. Look for KPIs that are red but strategically important. 3. Compare one red KPI with
one near-target or over-target KPI to determine whether the next action should increase activity volume or improve
orchestration quality.
When reach is healthy but orchestration is weak, do not ask teams to 'just do more touches'. Improve sequencing, tighten
follow-up rules, and redesign priority-account journeys so channels work together rather than as disconnected touchpoints.

3. Decision pattern: value leaks after the touchpoint
Event Follow-up Rate and Promotional Email Effectiveness are process-discipline KPIs. They reveal whether
engagement is carried forward after the moment of contact. In this slice, follow-up hovers around target in the largest
markets but drops materially in the lower-performing markets, while promotional email effectiveness remains below its
target almost everywhere.
1. Open the event follow-up visual first and check whether low follow-up is broad-based or market-specific. 2. Compare
it with promotional email effectiveness. 3. If both are weak, the problem is not only attendance or send volume; it is the
quality of post-touch execution.
Treat this pattern as a value-leakage problem. The right response is tighter event-close workflows, better next-best-action rules
after attendance, and stronger linkage between event activity and approved digital follow-up.

4. Decision pattern: high touch pressure, weak engagement
quality
Interaction Frequency tells you whether the team is showing up often enough. Multi-Channel Engagement Score tells
you whether those interactions are working together. When interaction frequency is close to plan but the multi-channel
score remains weak, leadership should resist the temptation to push even more volume.
Questions to ask: Are TC1 accounts receiving repetitive touches through the same channel? Are channels sequenced
intentionally, or are they simply accumulating activity? Does the follow-up flow convert one interaction into the next, or
are customers experiencing isolated contacts?
Treat this pattern as an orchestration problem, not automatically as an access problem. The corrective lever is better journey
design: which channel comes first, what should follow, and how quickly should the next touch happen for priority accounts.

5. Decision pattern: prioritize markets with both size and
underperformance
Engagement leaders should not chase the reddest KPI alone. The correct approach is to combine market size with the
type of engagement weakness. Germany and France, for example, may have acceptable digital reach but still weak
orchestration scores. Smaller markets may be redder, but they do not always deserve first intervention if the
commercial impact is lower.
1. Sort markets by engagement-relevant scale, such as target universe, interaction volume, or priority-account count.
2. Select the first large market with weak engagement quality. 3. Check whether the same weakness repeats across
multiple major markets. 4. Escalate only after confirming that the issue is strategic, not just local noise.
By the end of the review, the user should be able to name the top two engagement weaknesses, the top three markets to
prioritize, and the two levers most likely to improve the next quarter: for example, stronger event follow-up and better
multi-channel choreography.

 Definitions & Calculations
 The KPI logic behind the engagement cards and scorecard, with dataset-specific formulas, worked

Document purpose
This guide is designed for realistic end-user enablement on the Customer Engagement Dashboard in the NovaCarta EMEA
dummy dataset. It explains what the core engagement KPIs mean, how they are calculated, how to interpret them in human
terms, and how to use them together rather than in isolation. The KPI set is grounded in the NovaCarta business and technical
documentation, where Digital Channel Coverage, Email Utilization Rate, Event Follow-up Rate, Interaction Frequency TC1 and
TC2, Promotional Email Effectiveness, and Multi-Channel Engagement Score are defined as the core customer-engagement
measures.

NovaCarta Pharma | Customer Engagement Dashboard KPI Definitions & Calculations
Page 2
1. Digital Channel Coverage
Digital Channel Coverage measures how much of the target HCP universe is reached through digital channels. In the
NovaCarta framework, digital means web calls plus approved email activity. It is a breadth KPI: it answers whether
digital reach exists at all, not whether the interaction was deep or compelling.
Business definition
Percentage of target universe reached via digital channels (web + email).
Digital Channel Coverage = DIVIDE(Distinct HCPs reached by WEB or APPROVED_EMAIL, Total target HCP
universe)
Worked example
If 512 target HCPs were reached digitally out of a target universe of 1,000, the result is 512 ÷ 1,000 = 51.2%. That
mirrors the FY2025 Q4 reference value used in the Executive materials.
Human-friendly interpretation
This KPI answers the question: did our digital channels actually reach the people we intended to reach? A value above target is
reassuring, but it should never be confused with engagement quality. A team can hit digital reach and still perform poorly on
orchestration and follow-up.

2. Email Utilization Rate
Email Utilization Rate focuses on the proportion of consented HCPs who actually receive an approved email. It sits
inside the digital category, but it is narrower than Digital Channel Coverage because it depends on consent status and
approved-email execution.
Business definition
Percentage of consented HCPs receiving at least one approved email.
Email Utilization Rate = DIVIDE(Consented HCPs with at least one APPROVED_EMAIL interaction, Total
consented HCPs)
Worked example
If 448 consented HCPs receive at least one approved email out of 1,000 consented HCPs, the result is 44.8%. Against a
70.0% target, that means the channel is under-used even before engagement quality is assessed.
Human-friendly interpretation
This KPI tells you whether the team is using the approved-email capability enough. A low value does not automatically mean the
emails are ineffective. It often means the emails are simply not being deployed widely enough among consented accounts.

3. Event Follow-up Rate and Promotional Email Effectiveness
These two KPIs are often read together because they both test whether engagement continues after an outreach
moment. Event Follow-up Rate checks whether event attendance is converted into post-event action. Promotional Email
Effectiveness checks whether emails generate documented engagement after delivery.
Business definitions
 Event Follow-up Rate = percentage of event attendees receiving follow-up within 14 days.
 Promotional Email Effectiveness = percentage of promotional emails with documented engagement.
Event Follow-up Rate = DIVIDE(Event attendees receiving follow-up within 14 days, Total event
attendees)
Promotional Email Effectiveness = DIVIDE(Promotional emails with documented engagement, Total
promotional emails sent)
Worked example
If 593 of 1,000 event attendees receive follow-up, the follow-up rate is 59.3%. If 187 promotional emails out of 1,000
sent show documented engagement, effectiveness is 18.7%. Both examples show a common pattern: activity exists,
but value leaks after the touchpoint.
Human-friendly interpretation
These KPIs answer whether the team is closing the loop. Good events with weak follow-up waste momentum. High email sends
with low effectiveness create noise rather than progression. In customer engagement reviews, these are classic
process-discipline indicators.

4. Interaction Frequency and Multi-Channel Engagement Score
Interaction Frequency is the simplest intensity KPI: how often are priority accounts actually engaged? The
Multi-Channel Engagement Score is the more sophisticated partner metric: how well are different touchpoints combined
into a meaningful engagement pattern?
Business definitions
 Interaction Frequency TC1 = average interactions per high-potential account in the reporting period.
 Interaction Frequency TC2 = average interactions per medium-potential account in the reporting period.
 Multi-Channel Engagement Score = weighted composite score of multi-channel touchpoints per HCP.
Interaction Frequency TC1 = DIVIDE(Total TC1 interactions, Distinct TC1 accounts)
Interaction Frequency TC2 = DIVIDE(Total TC2 interactions, Distinct TC2 accounts)
Multi-Channel Engagement Score = Weighted composite of distinct channels, touchpoint sequence, and
recency per HCP
Worked example
If 520 interactions are delivered to 100 TC1 accounts, the average interaction frequency is 5.2. If the engagement model
scores each HCP across channel diversity and sequencing and the resulting average is 2.3 against a 7.5 target, the
organization is touching accounts often enough to register activity but not coordinating those touches well enough to
create depth.
Human-friendly interpretation
Interaction Frequency tells you whether teams are showing up. Multi-Channel Engagement Score tells you whether those
interactions feel connected. A strong customer-engagement dashboard needs both, because frequency without orchestration
often becomes over-contacting with limited impact.

5. Customer engagement KPI scorecard metrics
The customer-engagement scorecard is where the dashboard shifts from observation to diagnosis. Each KPI is read
with its target so leaders can separate a simple execution shortfall from a deeper orchestration problem.
KPI Value = AVERAGE(FACT_KPI_Summary[kpi_value]) or KPI-specific aggregate from the source fact table
KPI Target = AVERAGE(FACT_KPI_Summary[target_value]) or configured target from KPI framework
KPI % Attainment = DIVIDE([KPI Value], [KPI Target])
How to read the scorecard
 Digital Channel Coverage at 102.4% attainment means reach is slightly above target.
 Event Follow-up Rate at 98.8% attainment is close to target, but still leaking value after events.
 Interaction Frequency TC1 at 86.7% attainment and TC2 at 90.0% attainment mean account pressure is present, but
not fully at plan.
 Promotional Email Effectiveness at 74.8% attainment shows the team is generating less documented email
engagement than needed.
 Multi-Channel Engagement Score at 30.7% attainment is the standout warning signal: channels are being used, but
not orchestrated well enough to create meaningful depth.
Human-friendly interpretation
This scorecard should stop managers from asking only 'are we active enough?' It pushes them to ask the better question: 'are our
touchpoints combining into a coherent customer journey?' When reach is green but orchestration is red, the correct response is
not more volume. It is better sequencing, stronger follow-up, and sharper account planning.

Reference scope
This document summarizes the core customer-engagement KPIs used in the NovaCarta training materials. It is designed as a quick-reference companion for dashboard users, analysts, and enablement teams.
KPI Name | KPI Definition | KPI Calculation | Data Source
Digital Channel Coverage | Percentage of the target HCP universe reached through digital channels. In this dashboard, digital reach combines approved email and web-based interactions. | Digital HCPs Reached ÷ Total Target HCP Universe | FACT_Coverage (kpi006_digital_coverage_pct) derived from FACT_Interactions flags for web and email, plus target universe fields in FACT_Coverage.
Email Utilization Rate | Percentage of consented HCPs who received at least one approved promotional or informational email in the selected period. | Consented HCPs with ≥1 approved email ÷ Total Consented HCPs | FACT_Interactions (is_email_flag / kpi014_promo_email_flag), filtered by consent logic from DIM_Account[consent_status].
Event Follow-up Rate | Percentage of HCPs who attended an event and then received a documented follow-up interaction within the expected follow-up window. | Event Attendees with Follow-up ÷ Total Event Attendees | FACT_Interactions using is_event_flag and kpi008_event_followup_flag.
Interaction Frequency TC1 | Average number of completed interactions delivered to high-priority target-class-1 accounts during the selected period. | Total TC1 Interactions ÷ Distinct TC1 Accounts | FACT_Interactions using kpi012_tc1_interaction_flag; target class sourced from FACT_Account_Metrics[target_class].
Interaction Frequency TC2 | Average number of completed interactions delivered to medium-priority target-class-2 accounts during the selected period. | Total TC2 Interactions ÷ Distinct TC2 Accounts | FACT_Interactions using kpi013_tc2_interaction_flag; target class sourced from FACT_Account_Metrics[target_class].

Multi-Channel Engagement Score | Weighted score that reflects how well multiple engagement channels are combined for the same HCP or account. This KPI is intended to measure orchestration quality, not just channel volume. | Weighted Composite of Channel Diversity, Sequence, and Recency | FACT_Interactions using kpi015_multichannel_score, supported by channel flags (F2F, web, email, event).
