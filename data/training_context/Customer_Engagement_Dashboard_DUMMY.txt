Customer Engagement Dashboard
KPI Master Table
Business definitions, calculations, and data sources for the NovaCarta customer engagement KPI set
Use this file as the Word equivalent of the KPI spreadsheet: one place to see what each KPI means, how it is calculated, and which dataset elements support it.
Customer Engagement KPI Reference
The table below follows the same logic as the spreadsheet version, but is formatted for human-readable training and documentation. Definitions are written in business language first, calculations are written in dashboard-friendly terms, and the data-source column shows where the KPI is sourced in the NovaCarta model.
Reference scope
This document summarizes the core customer-engagement KPIs used in the NovaCarta training materials. It is designed as a quick-reference companion for dashboard users, analysts, and enablement teams.
KPI Name | KPI Definition | KPI Calculation | Data Source
Digital Channel Coverage | Percentage of the target HCP universe reached through digital channels. In this dashboard, digital reach combines approved email and web-based interactions. | Digital HCPs Reached ÷ Total Target HCP Universe | FACT_Coverage (kpi006_digital_coverage_pct) derived from FACT_Interactions flags for web and email, plus target universe fields in FACT_Coverage.
Email Utilization Rate | Percentage of consented HCPs who received at least one approved promotional or informational email in the selected period. | Consented HCPs with ≥1 approved email ÷ Total Consented HCPs | FACT_Interactions (is_email_flag / kpi014_promo_email_flag), filtered by consent logic from DIM_Account[consent_status].
Event Follow-up Rate | Percentage of HCPs who attended an event and then received a documented follow-up interaction within the expected follow-up window. | Event Attendees with Follow-up ÷ Total Event Attendees | FACT_Interactions using is_event_flag and kpi008_event_followup_flag.
Interaction Frequency TC1 | Average number of completed interactions delivered to high-priority target-class-1 accounts during the selected period. | Total TC1 Interactions ÷ Distinct TC1 Accounts | FACT_Interactions using kpi012_tc1_interaction_flag; target class sourced from FACT_Account_Metrics[target_class].
Interaction Frequency TC2 | Average number of completed interactions delivered to medium-priority target-class-2 accounts during the selected period. | Total TC2 Interactions ÷ Distinct TC2 Accounts | FACT_Interactions using kpi013_tc2_interaction_flag; target class sourced from FACT_Account_Metrics[target_class].
Promotional Email Effectiveness | Share of promotional emails that generated a documented engagement signal, such as interaction continuation, click-through proxy, or follow-up outcome. | Promotional Emails with Engagement ÷ Total Promotional Emails Sent | FACT_Interactions using kpi014_promo_email_flag and related email / follow-up engagement markers.
Multi-Channel Engagement Score | Weighted score that reflects how well multiple engagement channels are combined for the same HCP or account. This KPI is intended to measure orchestration quality, not just channel volume. | Weighted Composite of Channel Diversity, Sequence, and Recency | FACT_Interactions using kpi015_multichannel_score, supported by channel flags (F2F, web, email, event).
