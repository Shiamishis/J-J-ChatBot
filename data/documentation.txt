1. Data Model Overview
The NovaCarta EMEA CDP implements a star schema with 6 dimension tables and 8 fact tables. Key design feature: brands can have multiple indications (DIM_Brand_Indication bridge), enabling brand-level sales aggregation with indication-level interaction tracking.
1.1 Table Inventory
Table	Type	Primary Key	Records	Description
DIM_Geography	Dimension	geo_id	287	Five-level geographic hierarchy for EMEA
DIM_Brand	Dimension	brand_id	15	Brand master data (15 brands across 4 TAs)

DIM_Time	Dimension	time_id	36	Calendar time: Jan 2023 - Dec 2025

5. Generation Methodology
Seed: 42 | Names: Faker (locale-appropriate per country)
Revenue: Parameterized trajectory functions per indication with seasonal + Gaussian noise
Interactions: Per employee/month, weighted by prescriber_volume, with indication selection weighted by revenue size
Coverage/Performance: Derived from atomic interactions for consistency
Targets: Revenue trajectory * country weight * 1.05 uplift; coverage by lifecycle stage
